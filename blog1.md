# Blog 1: What Is Data Science to Me?

Data science to me is essentially like finding a method to the madness. The method can be whatever lane you choose to make sense of the data you are working with, whether it be SQL, Python, or even machine learning. You are basically tasked with figuring out which question is worth asking, then communicating what you found.

After starting to work on my first project in DTSC2301 I felt like this has mostly reaffirmed what I thought. A lot of what we do as data scientists is really just organization. We need to make sense of the numbers and what is going on with the information, and we also need to assist others in doing so. 

I feel like communication is the part people may not consider as much. You can run the most impressive model in the world, but if you can't explain what it means to someone who wasn't working with you, it doesn't matter much. A chart or a summary only works if it gives the reader something they can act on. In that sense, a data scientist is like part analyst and part translator. This is also why I want to get better at asking sharper questions at the start of a project.
