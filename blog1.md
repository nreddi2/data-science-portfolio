# Blog 1: What Is Data Science to Me?

Data is everywhere and everything in today’s world. Everything is documented nowadays, so yes, everything quite literally has data attached to it. There is so much going on with it to the point where there needs to be people who can not only find the method to all of the madness, but be able to properly convey it. That is why data science is so truly important right now. We need a mediator; someone who can take data, examine it, clean it, analyze it, and then make sense of it for everyone else to understand.

I feel like this topic is really interesting, mostly because data science is a relatively new field for one. It is really a culmination of several concentrations, namely statistics and computer science. I would say to me it really is a means of utilizing what we know to answer impactful questions. 

After starting to work on my first project in DTSC2301 I felt like this has mostly reaffirmed what I thought. Obviously, there’s different processes involved with data, such as cleaning, examining, and modeling. But a lot of what we do as data scientists is really just organization. We need to make sense of the numbers and what is going on with the information, and we also need to assist others in doing so.
