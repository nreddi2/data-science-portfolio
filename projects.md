## Exploring Gender Inequality in Wages in North Carolina

This DTSC 2301 project uses the 2024 American Community Survey (ACS) 1-Year Public Use Microdata Sample (PUMS) API to explore differences in wage and salary income by reported sex and educational attainment among working-age North Carolina residents.
